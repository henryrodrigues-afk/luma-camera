This version uses InteractiveSegmenter with a normalized point RegionOfInterest.
The v1 asset has float32 input [1,512,512,4] and a single foreground
sigmoid probability output [1,512,512,1]. The app uses confidence masks,
not category-mask numeric labels or qualityScores as confidence.

LumaCamera bundles the model and runs it locally. No model download,
image upload or Internet permission is needed by the application.
The legacy model is intended for interactive still-image segmentation.
Live use in this app repeats single-frame inference at a maximum of three
submissions per second with assisted interior reseeding. This does not
provide optical flow, depth estimation or stable object identity tracking.
Small targets, adjacent objects, occlusions, noise and rapid movement can
reduce mask quality or lose the selected target. A new tap may be needed.
After a lost or invalid mask, this app stops analysis for that selection
until a new explicit tap or mode change. It does not automatically claim
that a different object at the old point is the original selected target.
